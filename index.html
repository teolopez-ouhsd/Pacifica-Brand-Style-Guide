Style Guide

How to use the Pacifica Tritons logos, colors, and type consistently across print, web, apparel, uniforms, presentations, and AI-generated materials.

Everyone creating Pacifica materials, including staff, coaches, clubs, ASB, and boosters, should check this page before designing anything new.

02 Clear Space & Sizex = cap height of PACIFICA

Clear Space

Keep the area around the logo free of text, photos, other logos, and the edge of the page so it can breathe and stay legible.

xxxx

Minimum clear space on every side equals x, the height of the letters in PACIFICA (the small top line of the word mark). For marks without lettering, such as the trident or shield, use one quarter of the mark's width. When the logo is resized, the clear space scales with it.

03 Usage Rules4 do8 don't

Protect the Marks

Proportions, colors, and construction are fixed. If you find yourself dragging a corner handle, changing a fill color, or adding an effect, stop and grab a different file from the library instead.

✓ Do

Use the full-color logo on white or light backgrounds whenever color printing is available.

✓ Do

On Pacifica Green, use the trident or Triton figure. Their built-in white fills keep them visible.

✓ Do

The Secondary Logo (Triton) holds up on dark gray and black for athletics and spirit graphics.

✓ Do

Use the Shield for square spaces: social avatars, stickers, patches, and app icons.

✕ Don't

Stretch, squash, or distort. Hold Shift when resizing.

✕ Don't

Recolor. Green, gold, gray, and black only, as supplied.

✕ Don't

Add shadows, glows, outlines, bevels, or other effects.

✕ Don't

Rotate or tilt the logo.

✕ Don't

Put word marks or lettered logos on green or dark backgrounds. The letters have no outline and disappear.

04 ColorPantone · CMYK · RGB · HEXClick any value to copy

Color Palette

Pacifica Green leads. Gold is an accent used sparingly, the way it appears only in the Triton's crown and armor. Gray, Dark Gray, and Black support.

Pacifica Green · shades & tints

Pacifica Gold · shades & tints

Base vs. tints

Base colors are for primary brand moments: logos, headers, uniforms, signage. Shades and tints are for supporting work such as web UI, charts, table fills, and backgrounds. For print, match the official Pantone or CMYK values.

Pantone

Pantone colors are official. Give print vendors the PMS number for spot-color jobs and the CMYK values for 4-color printing. On-screen colors may not match Pantone standards exactly; consult a current Pantone guide for color-critical work.

06 AccessibilityWCAG 2.24.5:1 body · 3:1 large text & UI

Contrast

WCAG requires at least 4.5:1 contrast for normal body text, and at least 3:1 for large text (18px and up, or 14px bold) and UI elements like icons, buttons, and input borders. Ratios below are calculated from the official HEX values.

ColorAs text on whiteAs text on blackWhite text on itBlack text on it
Never

Gold text on Green, or Green on Gold. The pair measures about 2.4:1, well under the 3:1 minimum. It looks bold on a jersey but is unreadable as text. Separate them with white, black, or plenty of space.

Avoid

Gold and Gray as text on white (2.9:1 and 2.4:1). Use them for accents, rules, and icons beside text, never for the words themselves. Both read well on black.

  • Pacifica Green on white passes AA for all text sizes. Use it for headings and links.
  • Use Pacifica Green, or a Green shade, as button backgrounds with white text.
  • Avoid light tints behind white text; use them as subtle fills behind dark text.
  • Underline links regardless of color, so color isn't the only signal.

Open Sans

Paragraphs, captions, letters, forms, tables, and website body copy. Regular 400 and SemiBold 600, with Italic for light emphasis. Matches the district's body text.

Installing Triton Block
  • Mac: double-click each .otf file, then click Install Font.
  • Windows: right-click each .otf file and choose Install for all users.
  • Websites and Wix: upload the .woff2 files as custom fonts.
  • Canva or Adobe Express: upload the .otf files as brand fonts.
  • Small caps: type lowercase, or use the c2sc feature to turn capitals into small caps.
Important

Triton Block does not replace the logo. Never type PACIFICA TRITONS in Triton Block as a stand-in for the word mark, and don't use it for uniform numbers. Always place the official logo and numeral files from the Logo Library. It is a display face; don't set paragraphs in it.

08 Name & Mascot

Writing the Name

✓ Do
  • Use Pacifica High School on first reference in formal writing, then Pacifica.
  • The team name is Tritons: capital T, plural. One athlete or the mascot figure is a Triton.
  • Write Go Tritons! or Pacifica Tritons in spirit copy.
  • Include Oxnard Union High School District on official documents, letterhead, and forms.
✕ Don't
  • Write Titans. It's an easy typo on rosters and flyers, and spell-check won't catch it.
  • Use a lowercase "tritons" or put the name in quotes.
  • Type the team name in place of a logo; place the word mark file instead.
09 AI & FlyersArtwork only · real logo · real fonts

AI Image Tools

Image generators can't reproduce the Triton exactly. Ask one to "include the Pacifica logo" and it invents a new, slightly wrong Triton: extra trident prongs, a different crown, made-up lettering. Use AI for the artwork only, then add the real logo and text yourself.

  1. Generate artwork onlyBackgrounds, illustration, or photo-style scenes. No logos, mascots, text, or seals.
  2. Leave clear spaceAsk for empty, calm space in a corner or along one edge where the logo will go.
  3. Place the real logoDrop the official PNG or SVG into Canva, Adobe Express, Slides, or Word.
  4. Add text by handSet headlines in Triton Block and body copy in Open Sans.
✕ AvoidCreate a flyer for Pacifica High School's homecoming with the Tritons logo and school colors.

The tool will draw a fake Triton and guess at "school colors."

✓ Use insteadGenerate a bright, photographic stadium-at-night background in deep green #006747 with warm gold #CC8A00 light. Leave empty negative space in the upper third. No text, no logos, no mascots.

Hex codes, not color names. Then add the logo and words yourself.

  • Never ask an AI tool to draw, recreate, or "improve" the Triton, the trident, or the shield.
  • Never upload the logo as a reference image for an AI tool to imitate.
  • Confirm clear space and contrast before you publish.
